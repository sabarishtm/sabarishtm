Help

Everything I sing shows as "off". Your Sa setting probably does not match your voice. Open Me → Change Sa, run Find your Sa, and sing your Sa when asked. If your whole take is off by one steady amount, the report will suggest the Sa you were singing.

It cannot hear me. Allow the microphone in iOS Settings → Paatu Vaathiyar (on Android: Settings → Apps → Paatu Vaathi → Permissions), and try headphones.

What is a kattai, a kalam, a sama? Tap or hold any underlined word in the app, or open Me → Glossary.

Does it record me? No. Your voice is analysed on the phone and no audio is saved.
