Paatu Vaathiyar: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

The short version: your voice is analysed on your iPhone and never leaves it. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it collects no personal information.

Your voice and the microphone

Paatu Vaathiyar uses the microphone only while you practise, to work out the pitch you are singing and to compare it with the notes of the lesson. This analysis happens in real time on your device. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

What the app keeps on your device

To remember where you are, the app stores on your iPhone: your settings (your Sa, language, tempo and similar choices), your lesson progress, and, for the latest take of each lesson, the pitch readings and note scores shown in its report. This is never uploaded and is removed when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for this app, and it contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Links

Some "read more" links open external websites inside the app. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Apple

If you installed the app through TestFlight, or you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous, aggregated crash and usage data. It cannot be used to identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions or requests: message @PaatuVaathiyar on Instagram or on X.
